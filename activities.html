Butterfly Activities

Get creative and do hands-on activities! Make a life cycle wheel, move like each stage, and draw a butterfly-friendly garden.

Three Activities to Try

Activity 1: Make a Life Cycle Wheel

Create a colorful wheel that shows the four stages of a butterfly's life.

Materials

  • Paper or a paper plate
  • Crayons or colored pencils
  • Pencil
  • Scissors (ask an adult for help)
1Divide your paper into four sections.
2Draw one stage in each section: egg, larva, pupa, and adult butterfly.
3Add arrows to show the order of the stages.
4Label each stage and color your life cycle wheel.
5Share your wheel with a classmate and explain the changes.

Activity 2: Butterfly Movement Game

Move like each stage of the butterfly life cycle!

Egg
Curl up like an egg
Larva
Wiggle gently like a caterpillar
Pupa
Stay still like a pupa
Adult
Slowly flap your arms like a butterfly
Safety first: Move safely and keep enough space around you.

Activity 3: Draw a Butterfly-Friendly Garden

Design a garden where butterflies can live and visit.

1Draw a garden with flowers and suitable plants.
2Add a butterfly visiting a flower.
3Label two ways people can care for butterflies and their habitat.
Think about: What plants do caterpillars need? What flowers do adult butterflies like?
Reminder:

"Be kind to nature. Butterflies remind us that even the smallest changes can make a big difference!"

Little steps. Big change. A healthier planet for tomorrow!
