ITS IMPORTANCE · WHY ARE BUTTERFLIES IMPORTANT?

More Than Just Beautiful

Butterflies are more than beautiful insects. They are part of nature and help keep ecosystems healthy.

From pollinating flowers to feeding other animals, butterflies play an important role in nature. Protecting them helps protect the whole environment.

Why Are Butterflies Important?

1

They Help Pollinate Flowers

Pollen can be carried from one flower to another when butterflies visit flowers. This helps plants make seeds and fruit.

2

They Are Part of the Food Web

Butterflies and caterpillars are food for some birds and other animals.

3

They Support Biodiversity

They are one of many living things that make an environment rich and balanced.

4

They Help Us Observe Nature

Changes in butterfly numbers and types can give scientists clues about the condition of habitats.

Why Should We Protect Them?

Butterflies need safe places with flowering plants and host plants for caterpillars. Protecting their habitats helps butterflies and many other living things.

Reminders: Be Kind to Nature

1

Observe butterflies gently. Do not chase, catch, or harm them.

2

Do not damage caterpillars, eggs, or the plants where they live.

3

Plant and care for suitable flowers and native plants with an adult's guidance.

4

Keep gardens and outdoor spaces clean. Put rubbish in the proper bin.

5

Avoid disturbing butterfly habitats and never remove living things from the wild just for fun.

6

Share what you learn and encourage others to care for nature.

A Little Reminder

"Be kind to nature. Butterflies remind us that even small changes can make a big difference!"

Little steps. Big change. A healthier planet for tomorrow!
