Butterfly Games

Play these fun games to practice what you know about the butterfly life cycle. Tap the cards, type your guesses, and make your matches!

Three Games to Try

Game 1: Put the Stages in Order

Tap a word card to put it in the next slot. Tap a card in a slot to send it back.

1
2
3
4

Challenge: Write the correct order in your notebook, too!

Game 2: Who Am I?

Read each clue and type your guess. Then tap Check!

Clue 1: I am tiny and can be found on a plant. What am I?
Clue 2: I have a long body, many legs, and I eat leaves. What am I?
Clue 3: I am the stage when the insect changes inside a protective covering. What am I?
Clue 4: I have wings and can visit flowers. What am I?

Game 3: Butterfly Match-Up

Tap a word on the left, then tap its description on the right. Check your matches when you're done!

Reminder:

"Be kind to nature. Butterflies remind us that even the smallest changes can make a big difference!"
